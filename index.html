Spreadsheet change control · Runs in your browser

An AI edited your workbook. Which formulas did it break?

Copilots and agents rewrite spreadsheets fast — and quietly paste values over formulas, shorten SUM ranges, wrap errors in IFERROR and change constants. Sheet Sentry compares the two files cell by cell, understands inserted rows, and tells you what needs a second look.

Nothing is uploaded. Workbooks are unzipped and compared inside this tab.

What it catches

Critical

Hardcoded formulas

A formula replaced by a number — often its own last result, so it looks right today and never updates again.

Critical

Shrunken ranges

SUM(B2:B6) became SUM(B2:B5). Checked after accounting for inserted rows. Deletion-affected references need manual review.

Critical

New errors

#REF!, #DIV/0! and lost references the edit introduced.

High

Masked errors

Formulas newly wrapped in IFERROR or IFNA that turn problems into zeros.

High

Changed constants

A growth rate of 1.05 that became 1.08 inside a formula.

High

Redefined names

Named ranges pointing at a different cell, links to other workbooks, macros.

Medium

Inputs, hidden rows, hidden sheets

Typed values that changed, and content made invisible.

Info

Recalculated results

Outputs that moved only because their inputs did — listed, not alarmed.
